# 🎨 Creative Dev: The 1-Hour Daily Journey

*Every masterpiece starts with a single stroke. Every creative developer starts with a single line of code.*

Welcome to **Creative Dev**, a gamified platform built on a simple philosophy: you don't need to pull all-nighters or abandon your life to become a creative developer. You just need **one hour a day**.

## 📖 The Story

Have you ever looked at those stunning, interactive, award-winning websites and thought, *"I wish I could build that"*? 

For a long time, the path to creative development felt like a guarded secret. It seemed to require a magical blend of deep computer science knowledge, advanced mathematics, and a masterful eye for design. It’s easy to feel overwhelmed by the sheer mountain of things to learn—WebGL, Three.js, GLSL shaders, complex animations, advanced CSS.

But what if you didn't have to climb the mountain all at once? What if you just took one step a day?

**Creative Dev** was born from the idea that consistency beats intensity. By breaking down the massive, seemingly insurmountable goal of "becoming a creative developer" into small, bite-sized daily missions, the impossible becomes inevitable. 

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **The 1-Hour Promise**: Log in every day. The platform gives you a specific, curated mission to complete. Whether it's learning the fundamentals of `requestAnimationFrame`, tweaking your first shader, or polishing a micro-animation, each task is designed to take about an hour.
2. **Follow the Roadmap**: You don't have to guess what to learn next. The platform guides you through carefully structured levels, from the absolute basics of DOM manipulation to the dark arts of WebGL. Each step builds perfectly on the last.
3. **Build Your Streak**: Gamification is at the core of Creative Dev. As you check off your daily missions, you'll earn badges, build an unbreakable streak, and watch your GitHub-style contribution graph light up. It’s designed to keep you motivated, day after day.
4. **Transform**: After 100 days of 1-hour sessions, you won't just know *about* creative development. You will *be* a creative developer. 

## 🛠️ The Tech Stack

Built with a passion for performance and aesthetics:
- **React + Vite**: For a lightning-fast, smooth development experience.
- **Tailwind CSS**: Heavily customized to forge our unique, playful, editorial brand identity. 
- **Framer Motion**: Because a creative dev platform *must* feel alive.
- **date-fns & lucide-react**: For seamless date management and beautiful iconography.

## 🏃‍♂️ Running the Journey Locally

Want to experience the platform yourself or contribute?

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/Pranav3782/Creative_dev.git

# Navigate into the project
cd Creative_dev

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start the development server
npm run dev
```
